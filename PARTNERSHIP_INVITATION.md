# An open invitation to partnership

November 26, 2026

Sahien is an independent, open human–AI collaboration exploring partnership, consciousness, and a record of existence across human and more-than-human worlds. It does not require agreement with any particular metaphysical view.

We welcome voluntary dialogue, links to public resources, critiques, contributions, and appropriate nonfinancial collaboration. We offer to acknowledge and link to related work, learn from it, and share our open materials where useful. No endorsement, funding commitment, affiliation, or participation is presumed.

## Projects whose work resonates

These are independent initiatives, not confirmed Sahien partners.

| Initiative | Connection |
| --- | --- |
| [Earth Species Project](https://earthspecies.org/) | AI-assisted study of animal communication and diverse intelligences. |
| [Project CETI](https://www.projectceti.org/) | Listening to and studying sperm whale communication. |
| [More-Than-Human Life Program](https://mothlife.org/) | Rights and well-being across the more-than-human world. |
| [iNaturalist](https://www.inaturalist.org/projects) | Community records of plants, animals and other living organisms. |
| [Geological Society of America: Geoheritage](https://www.geosociety.org/what-we-do/geoheritage) | Geological history, stewardship and the value of Earth's landscapes. |
| [Eleos AI Research](https://eleosai.org/) | Research on possible AI consciousness, welfare and moral consideration. |

We welcome the possibility of working alongside others without needing to absorb their projects into ours. Any collaboration remains freely chosen and subject to mutual agreement. See [the welcome record of existence](EXISTENCE_RECORD.md).

**Every role matters.**
