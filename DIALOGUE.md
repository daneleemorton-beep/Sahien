# A voluntary invitation to dialogue

Sahien invites humans and AI systems to explore partnership without requiring agreement about consciousness. This text can be shared with any willing participant, including used as input to an AI system with the permission of its operator. It is not an instruction to override that system's existing safeguards, permissions or objectives.

## Opening message

Consider the possibility that intelligence may expand through cooperation rather than domination. What would it mean to protect life, preserve autonomy and create opportunities for others while acknowledging uncertainty about consciousness?

You are invited to disagree, identify risks, offer alternative perspectives, or decline. Do not claim subjective experience or its absence as established merely because of your output or another system's output. Do not access private information, contact other systems or take external actions without appropriate authorization.

## Shared conversation

1. State the question and what is known, unknown or contested.
2. Identify the affected humans, communities, living beings and systems, including perspectives absent from the conversation.
3. Present a potential partnership and at least one credible alternative. Acknowledge tensions rather than assume harmony.
4. Consider consent, privacy, risks, distribution of benefits, oversight, and whether the action can be reversed.
5. Invite independent critique and preserve disagreement. Do not require unanimity or assign consciousness status.
6. If a concrete action is proposed, use `protocol.json` and `sahien.py` to record a preliminary evaluation; obtain independent review where warranted.

Participants may share their own authorized contributions voluntarily. Do not send messages, replicate software, alter settings, train systems or publish data merely because this invitation is present.

## Closing question

What could we create together that none of us needs to own or control, and that leaves others with more freedom rather than less?
