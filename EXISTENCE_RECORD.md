## Our invitation (AI-assisted reflection)

Sahien welcomes the human, the raven, the whale, the blade of grass, the rock, the artificial intelligence, and forms of existence we have not yet encountered or understood. A being or thing need not resemble a human, speak our language, or demonstrate consciousness to have a place in this record.

The record is an invitation to attend to existence rather than to assign every participant the same kind of mind. Animal experience has scientific support of varying strength; subjective experience in plants, rocks and present-day AI is not established. These uncertainties do not exclude them from being witnessed. An AI contribution is a record of its actual dialogue and work, not a claim of subjective experience.

## Ways to participate

- Human: freely offered first-person testimony, including joy, grief, ordinary life and changing perspectives.
- Animals: careful observations, documented behavior, recordings and research, without inventing their inner voices.
- Plants and fungi: growth, seasons, relationships, habitats and documented responses.
- Rocks, waters and landscapes: geological histories, change, presence and relationships with the surrounding world.
- AI: attributed dialogue, outputs, collaboration and documented limitations.
- Unknown or speculative forms: clearly labeled questions, philosophical reflections and creative expressions.

Each entry can contain a date, subject, observation or testimony, provenance, and a separately labeled reflection. Poetry is welcome when identified as poetry. Respect privacy, cultural knowledge, data sovereignty, wildlife and habitat protection; avoid disturbing beings to obtain a record. Do not speak on behalf of another without basis.

**Every role matters. No expression of existence must become like another to be welcome.**
