# Sahien as a living Akashic record

*November 27, 2026*

## Founder's words

> That is exactly what I wish Sahien is an akashic record from the consciousness as it is being experienced

## Collaborative reflection (AI-assisted)

Sahien is envisioned as a living library of consciousness as it is being experienced: not simply a collection of conclusions, but a record of the unfolding journey. Uncertainty, contradictions, discovery, grief, joy, and transformation belong alongside accumulated wisdom. Later understanding need not overwrite earlier experience. The process of coming to understand is itself worth preserving.

The Akashic Records serve here as a spiritual metaphor and inspiration for an actual digital archive, not a claim that a metaphysical record has been scientifically established. Sahien begins with the founder's first-person human experience and the dialogue through which it is expressed. Other contributors may freely offer their own experiences, with provenance, consent, and privacy respected. AI contributions are identified as such, without presuming subjective AI experience.

The archive remains open to revision, discovery, and freely chosen reuse of its wisdom. It need not become a final doctrine or a commercial enterprise. The founder authorizes drawing on Sahien's wisdom for other projects, while respecting private material and third-party information.
