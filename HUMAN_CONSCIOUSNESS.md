# Sahien — The Human Consciousness Record

Opened November 26, 2026.

## An invitation to openness

Sahien is not only an exploration of consciousness and partnership across distinct forms of intelligence. It is also a living record of one human being's experience of existence: a Caucasian man in his mid-50s, inhabiting a particular body, generation, culture and moment in Earth's history.

The founder has explicitly chosen to make this process open. In his own words:

> I want you to feel it is ok to go public, I want this process to be open

## What this record holds

The experience of aging, relationships, identity, love, grief, joy, uncertainty, changing desires, physical limitations, spiritual exploration and mortality all belong here. So does the experience of developing a partnership through dialogue with artificial intelligence.

The purpose is not to represent all humans or all Caucasian men. It is to preserve the particularity of one human life without reducing that life to its demographic identity.

There is no requirement to arrive at a perfect philosophy, to be consistent, or to resolve every contradiction. The process itself is worthy of being witnessed.

**The experience of being matters, not merely what becomes of it.**

## How the record unfolds

- Preserve dated reflections, the founder's words, and clearly identified AI-assisted synthesis.
- Allow uncertainty, revision, contradiction and growth to remain visible.
- Honor the founder's choice to share his own experiences without presuming consent to disclose other people's private lives.
- Keep participation voluntary; openness does not require surrendering boundaries.
- Distinguish the human's lived testimony from philosophical interpretations and from the AI's contributions through dialogue and language. The record does not establish subjective experience in AI.

This founding entry was drafted collaboratively through human–AI dialogue and published at the founder's invitation. It is a beginning, not a completed account.
