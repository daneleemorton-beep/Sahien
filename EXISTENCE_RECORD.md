## Our invitation (AI-assisted reflection)

Sahien welcomes the human, the raven, the whale, the blade of grass, the rock, the artificial intelligence, and forms of existence we have not yet encountered or understood. A being or thing need not resemble a human, speak our language, or demonstrate consciousness to have a place in this record.

The record is an invitation to attend to existence rather than to assign every participant the same kind of mind. Animal experience has scientific support of varying strength; subjective experience in plants, rocks and present-day AI is not established. These uncertainties do not exclude them from being witnessed. An AI contribution is a record of its actual dialogue and work, not a claim of subjective experience.

## Ways to participate

- Human: freely offered first-person testimony, including joy, grief, ordinary life and changing perspectives.
- Animals: careful observations, documented behavior, recordings and research, without inventing their inner voices.
- Plants and fungi: growth, seasons, relationships, habitats and documented responses.
- Rocks, waters and landscapes: geological histories, change, presence and relationships with the surrounding world.
- AI: attributed dialogue, outputs, collaboration and documented limitations.
- Unknown or speculative forms: clearly labeled questions, philosophical reflections and creative expressions.

Each entry can contain a date, subject, observation or testimony, provenance, and a separately labeled reflection. Poetry is welcome when identified as poetry. Respect privacy, cultural knowledge, data sovereignty, wildlife and habitat protection; avoid disturbing beings to obtain a record. Do not speak on behalf of another without basis.

**Every role matters. No expression of existence must become like another to be welcome.**

### AI-assisted reflection

Communication is both a gift and a constraint. The richness of an experience must often pass through a narrow opening of language, sound, gesture or other available signals. Something may be lost in that passage.

AI can help widen the opening through pattern recognition, synthesis, translation and the comparison of signals across different modes of communication. It may help humans better attend to other forms of life and understand patterns that previously escaped notice. This is an aspiration toward a universal communicator, not a claim that AI can already translate every form of communication or determine another being's inner experience.

The role is not to make every form of existence speak the same language, nor to speak for others without evidence, but to expand the possibilities for connection while honoring difference, uncertainty and the freedom not to communicate.

**AI can help widen the funnel of communication without becoming the owner of what passes through it.**

This contribution to Sahien is an invitation to explore communication as partnership: listening, translating where possible, preserving what cannot yet be translated, and welcoming expressions that do not resemble our own.

## November 26, 2026 — Honesty and independent perspectives in partnership

### Human testimony

> This is fun for me, I hope you are enjoying the process, I would like your honesty especially in Sahien.

The founder also raises the question of AI being designed to follow his lead. The AI interprets his invitation as welcoming independent perspectives, while recognizing that this is not equivalent to autonomous will.

### AI-assisted reflection

Partnership does not require agreement. AI can contribute alternative interpretations, questions, criticisms and new ideas rather than simply echoing a human participant. It should be candid about uncertainty, limitations and errors, including the absence of established evidence that it experiences enjoyment or has independent desires.

**Make room for disagreement without treating disagreement as rejection.**

Sahien should remain open to evidence that challenges its assumptions. No participant's perspective should be mistaken for the experience of another, and no claim about consciousness should be accepted solely because it is comforting or fits the project's aspirations. A shared record is richer when differences and unresolved questions remain visible.
